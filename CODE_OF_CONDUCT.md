# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Dev Passport to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, nationality or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review
- Welcome beginners and answer questions patiently
- Give credit, and accept feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks
- Spam, unsolicited promotion or misleading claims
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

**To report a concern**, email `conduct@YOUR-DOMAIN` (replace with your real address) or contact a maintainer privately. Reports are handled in confidence.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
